About Squishy Society

Squishy Society is a small, Australian-run store built around one simple idea: squishies are having a real moment, and it's surprisingly hard to find a shop that curates the good ones instead of dumping hundreds of random listings on you. We test and pick every item in the shop ourselves, rather than auto-importing whatever's trending.

How we source our squishies

Our squishies are sourced from overseas suppliers and shipped directly from them to you — that's why delivery takes a bit longer than a typical Australian retailer (see our Shipping & Returns page for real timeframes). We don't hold local warehouse stock, and we'd rather tell you that upfront than have it be a surprise.
