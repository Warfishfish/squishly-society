What we collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, email, shipping address, and order details. If you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your email address. We also collect basic analytics data (like which pages were visited) through our hosting provider.

How we use it

We use this information to process and ship your order, send you order and shipping updates, and respond if you contact us about a return or issue. We'll only send marketing emails if you've opted in via the newsletter signup — never otherwise.

Who we share it with

Your payment details go directly to our payment processor to charge your card — we never see or store your card details ourselves. Your name and shipping address are passed to our overseas supplier so they can fulfil and ship your order directly to you. Our website is hosted by Netlify, and basic traffic analytics may be processed through their platform.
